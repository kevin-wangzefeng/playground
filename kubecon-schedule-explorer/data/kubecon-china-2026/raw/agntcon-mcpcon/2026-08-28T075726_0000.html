Where the agentic stack is being built.

The event brings together developers, researchers, platform builders, and enterprises working across the agentic stack – from MCP and agent protocols to infrastructure, orchestration, and production systems.

Through deep technical sessions, real-world case studies, and ecosystem collaboration, AGNTCon + MCPCon explores how teams are building reliable, scalable, and secure agent systems in practice.

Each regional event connects local and global communities working to advance agentic AI systems at scale.

Who Attends? AGNTCon + MCPCon attracts a highly technical and influential audience, including:

  • Open source developers and contributors building MCP and other agentic AI projects
  • Project maintainers and foundation leaders guiding standards and governance
  • Enterprise engineers and architects adopting agent-based systems in production
  • Platform, infrastructure, and tooling teams supporting agent orchestration, security, and observability
  • Researchers and academics exploring the foundations and future of agentic systems

Attendees are practitioners actively shaping how agentic AI is designed, implemented, and deployed.
